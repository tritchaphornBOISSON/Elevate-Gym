# Elevate Gym brand

## Identity

- **Working name:** Elevate Gym
- **Descriptor:** Gym · Pattaya
- **Positioning:** a premium, modern gym brand that feels energetic,
  sophisticated, and unmistakably fitness-focused.

The name and descriptor are working copy until formally approved. Do not add
an opening date, history, credentials, or claims around them.

## Personality

The brand should feel disciplined, confident, refined, and active. Energy
comes from strong composition, scale, movement, and photography—not neon
effects, crowded graphics, or exaggerated claims.

Avoid the visual and verbal patterns of generic AI-generated fitness sites:
glowing gradients, fake metrics, motivational clichés, implausibly perfect
facility imagery, excessive cards, and software-style feature grids.

## Visual language

- **Deep charcoal/black:** primary ground and visual weight.
- **Warm cream:** welcoming contrast and the membership ribbon.
- **White:** clarity, copy, and high-contrast details.
- **Champagne:** the single restrained accent for italic emphasis, rules,
  outlines, arrows, focus indicators, and selected interaction details; never
  the dominant surface. Do not introduce a separate yellow/gold or peach
  accent treatment.

a premium, warm contrast and verify accessible text contrast before approval.

Use a bold sans-serif for headlines and an elegant italic serif as a limited
accent. Body copy should remain highly legible. Exact typefaces and licensing
remain **[To be confirmed: font families and usage rights]**.

## Photography

Choose editorial, high-contrast fitness photography with believable effort,
texture, equipment, and space. Crops should support the layout and preserve
the subject at all breakpoints. Do not imply specific facilities, equipment,
trainers, or accessibility features unless confirmed by supplied photography
and content.

set, credits, rights, focal points, and alt text]**.

## Voice

Write with concise confidence. Favor specific invitations and plain language
over hype. Never add unsupported superlatives, statistics, testimonials,
outcome promises, or performance claims.

Use explicit placeholders such as `[To be confirmed: opening hours]` instead
of invented copy when facts are missing.
